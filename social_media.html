Social media refers to online platforms and websites that allow individuals and businesses to create, share, and exchange information, ideas, and visual content over the internet. For new businesses, social media serves as a powerful tool to raise brand awareness, connect directly with prospective clients, and build a community around their products or services.

Social Media Comparison

Facebook

Facebook is one of the largest global social networks, allowing businesses to target diverse demographic groups through detailed advertising, business pages, and community groups.

Instagram

Instagram is a visual-centric platform focused on image and video sharing. It is highly effective for visual branding, influencer partnerships, and reaching younger audiences through stories and short-form reels.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn is the leading professional networking platform, ideal for business-to-business (B2B) marketing, corporate networking, industry thought leadership, and talent recruitment.
